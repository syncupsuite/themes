# Swiss International Style

> Sachlichkeit (Objectivity) — Precision, clarity, grid-based order.

## Story

Born from the Basel and Zurich schools of the 1950s, Swiss International Style prioritized objective clarity over decorative expression. Designers like Josef Muller-Brockmann and Armin Hofmann established principles of grid-based composition, asymmetric layouts, and sans-serif typography that became the global standard for corporate and informational design.

## Design Characteristics

- **Harmony mode**: Monochromatic — restrained, single-hue variations
- **Typography**: Neo-grotesque (`"Inter"`, `"Helvetica Neue"`)
- **Border radius**: None (sharp corners, geometric precision)
- **Neutrals**: Cool-tinted, minimal saturation
